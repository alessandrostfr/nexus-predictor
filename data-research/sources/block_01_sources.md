# Block 1 research sources

This document summarizes the first research pass used to seed the dataset.

## Reliability policy

Exact attendance per Nexus edition was not confirmed in the public sources found during this pass. For that reason, attendance values in the JSON files are stored as low-confidence seed ranges, not as confirmed facts. They will be recalculated later by the prediction block.
